Convergence Analysis

All three models show similar convergence patterns:

  • Initial Phase (1-50): Rapid learning, val loss drops from ~2.6-3.0 to ~1.2-1.7
  • Optimal Point (100-150): Best validation loss achieved
  • Overfitting Phase (200+): Train loss continues to decrease while val loss increases

Key Finding: Early Stopping Recommended

Early Stopping Recommendation

All models achieve their best validation loss at iteration 150 (~1.6 epochs). Continuing training beyond this point leads to overfitting. For production use, recommend training with early stopping at iteration 150-200.
